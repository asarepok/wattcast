R² (R-squared)
How much of the real change in output the model explains. 1.0 is a perfect match.
MAE (Mean Absolute Error)
The average size of the model's mistakes, in watts.
RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error)
Similar to MAE, but weighs large misses more heavily.
MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error)
The average mistake as a percentage of actual output.
